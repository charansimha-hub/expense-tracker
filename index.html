💡 Smart Savings Tips

1

The 50/30/20 Rule

Allocate 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings. This creates a balanced financial life.

2

24-Hour Rule

Before any non-essential purchase over ₹500, wait 24 hours. Most impulse purchases won't seem necessary the next day.

3

Meal Prep Sundays

Cooking meals at home saves 60-70% compared to eating out. Prep meals on Sunday for the whole week.

4

Audit Subscriptions

Review all recurring subscriptions monthly. Cancel unused ones — most people waste ₹500-2000/month on forgotten subscriptions.

5

Use Public Transport

Switch to public transport or carpooling 2-3 days a week. Save ₹3000-5000/month on fuel and parking.

6

Emergency Fund First

Build 3-6 months of expenses as emergency fund before investing. Use a high-interest savings account or liquid fund.

7

Automate Savings

Set up auto-debit on salary day to move savings amount first. "Pay yourself first" before spending on anything else.

8

No-Spend Challenge

Try 1 no-spend day per week. Only cover essentials. You'll discover how much you spend on things you don't need.
